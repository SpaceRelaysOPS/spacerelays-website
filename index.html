About Us

Connecting Space. Enabling Missions.

At SpaceRelays, we are building the next generation of space communication infrastructure - a scalable network of optical relay satellites in high Low Earth Orbit (LEO), delivering high-speed, real-time connectivity for satellites and space missions worldwide.

Traditional satellite communication often struggles with limited coverage and delayed data transmission. We're solving this with an advanced optical network built for reliable, continuous connectivity across space.

Built for Time-Sensitive Missions

Our infrastructure is designed for missions where speed, reliability, and data access matter most. Through our optical relay network, customers get faster, more efficient communication - without the cost and complexity of building their own infrastructure.

Our turnkey communication module makes integration simple, giving customers instant access to trusted, high-speed connectivity.
